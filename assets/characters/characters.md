# Characters, places and voice

Since v1.0 the characters and places are a shared sheet library in the data folder (`sheets/sheets.json`),
edited in the app (Settings → ตัวละครและสถานที่). The starting set is `assets/sheets.json`:
characters pinto, friend, mom, cat; places bedroom, living-room, bathroom, dining-room.
Stories add new tagged sheets (e.g. `teacher`, `school-classroom`) that later episodes reuse.

House theme (every picture): `assets/style.md` + simple solid black dot eyes for every character.
`origin.jpg` is the house style reference (drawing technique and colours only).
`pinto.jpg`, `friend.jpg`, `cat.jpg` are the pre-1.0 sheets (big eyes); they are no longer used for pictures
(`cat.jpg` is the app icon source).

## pinto — น้องปิ่นโต
- Role: the main character; leads every story. Narration calls her "ปิ่นโต".

## friend — เพื่อน (never named)
- Role: Pinto's friend. Narration calls him only "เพื่อน" or "เพื่อนของปิ่นโต".

## mom — แม่
- Role: Pinto's mother.

## cat — แมวดำ
- Role: Pinto's ordinary house pet, always with its red scarf; never part of the plot.

## Voice
- voice: `en-us-sola` (owner picked 2026-10-18 from achernar / vindemiatrix / en-us-sola / en-us-assistant-5)
- voice_style: A warm, cheerful young Thai woman reading a picture book aloud to children aged 4 to 10. Natural native Thai pronunciation and tones, smiling voice, calm and gentle, clear and a little slow, brief natural pauses between sentences.
